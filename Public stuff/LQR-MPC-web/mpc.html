MPC: look ahead, then choose

At every control step the MPC tries each candidate speed, predicts the next N steps through the curve ahead, and scores them. It keeps the best score, applies only the first move, and solves again 50 ms later.

Peak |e| is the largest predicted error over the horizon. A candidate that leaves the 6 mm sensor window pays a heavy penalty. The cost also charges for slowing below 150 mm/s, so MPC brakes only as much as it must.
