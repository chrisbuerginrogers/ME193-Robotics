LQR: one gain pair, worked out in advance

Steering is ω = −ke·e − kθ·θ. LQR picks the two gains from three numbers you choose: how much you mind lateral error (Qe), heading error (Qθ), and steering effort (R). Only the ratios matter.

Why a fixed gain fails on a sharp turn: ess = −κ·v / ke

On a constant curve the robot settles off the line by ess. When that exceeds the sensor window it goes blind. The last column is the fastest speed that keeps it inside the window.
